# Payments and billing

## Accepted payment methods
NordShop accepts Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and bank links of Swedbank, SEB and Citadele. Cash on delivery is not available.

## Double charge
A pending authorization can look like a double charge. Unconfirmed authorizations disappear automatically within 7 business days. If both charges are completed (not pending), support refunds the duplicate within 2 business days.

## Invoices
An invoice PDF is available in "My orders" right after the payment. Company details (VAT number, company name) can be added to an invoice only within 14 days after the purchase.

## Failed payment
If a payment fails, the order stays reserved for 24 hours so the customer can retry with another method. Support cannot see or change card details.
