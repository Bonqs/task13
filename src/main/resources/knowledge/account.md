# Account and security

## Password reset
The customer clicks "Forgot password" on the login page and receives a reset link that is valid for 1 hour. If the email does not arrive, ask the customer to check the spam folder and make sure they use the email address of the account.

## Locked account
After 5 failed login attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes. Support must never unlock an account or change its email address without verifying the customer's identity with the last order number and the delivery postcode.

## Suspicious activity
If a customer reports orders they did not place, the ticket must be escalated to the security team immediately with priority URGENT. Support asks the customer to reset the password but never asks for the password itself or full card details.

## Deleting an account
Customers can request account deletion in "Settings / Privacy". Data is deleted within 30 days according to GDPR; order history needed for accounting is kept for 5 years.
