# Refunds and returns

## Return window
Customers can return most items within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused and in its original packaging. Digital products (gift cards, software keys) cannot be returned once the code has been revealed.

## How to start a return
The customer opens "My orders", selects the order and clicks "Return item". A prepaid return label is emailed within 1 business day. Returns without a return number cannot be processed.

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after the warehouse receives the item. Banks may need another 3-5 business days to show the money. Store credit is issued instantly if the customer chooses it instead.

## Damaged or wrong item
If an item arrived damaged or the wrong item was delivered, the customer does not need to send it back first. Ask for a photo of the item and the packaging; a replacement or full refund including shipping costs is issued after the photo is checked.
