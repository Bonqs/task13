# Shipping and delivery

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 2-4 business days in the Baltics and 4-7 business days in the rest of the EU. Express delivery takes 1-2 business days and costs 9.99 EUR. Orders over 50 EUR ship for free with standard delivery.

## Tracking an order
A tracking link is emailed as soon as the parcel leaves the warehouse and is also shown in "My orders". Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.

## Late or lost parcel
If the parcel has not moved in tracking for 5 business days, it is treated as lost. Support opens an investigation with the carrier and the customer can choose a free re-shipment or a full refund without waiting for the investigation result.

## Changing the address
The delivery address can be changed only before the order is packed (status "Processing"). After the status changes to "Shipped" the address cannot be changed by support; the customer can redirect the parcel through the carrier's tracking page.
